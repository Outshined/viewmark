The owner decided on 2026-10-15, on 696d8kue c2, that the caret cannot stop on the blank line under a fence, a table, a diagram, an image, or a rule before the next block. That blank is space, as the blank between two paragraphs is (R7). 696d8kue sets the gap after each of those blocks to P.

Measured at 62f6923 in Chromium. Each file is a block, a blank line, then the paragraph `after`. The caret starts in `after`, and ↑ is pressed:
- A fence: the caret stops on the blank line, which is drawn as a line of its own.
- A rule: the same.
- A table: the same.
- Two paragraphs, for comparison: the caret goes to the paragraph above, past the space.

At 307fed5, QA measured the gap after a fence and after a rule as P plus one line, 39.5 px in Chromium, where the gap between two paragraphs is P.

## Acceptance
- {c1}In the rendered view, the caret cannot stop on the blank line between a fence, a table, a diagram, an image, or a rule and the block after it.{/c1} ↑ from the next block's first line goes to the last line of the block above, and ↓ goes back.
- The gap there is P, within 1 px, as 696d8kue sets it.
- A second blank line in a row is still a line the caret can take, as between paragraphs.
- In the raw view each of those lines stays a line.
- All of the above in Firefox.

Cells `BG.hr-p`, `BG.fc-p`, `BG.mm-p`, `BG.fc-fc`, `BG.tb-tb`, and `BG.p-hr` start with the caret on such a blank line. QA rewrites them, and adds a test named for this ticket, when this lands.
